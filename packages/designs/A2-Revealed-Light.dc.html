State orders audit of all bridges after Kesarganj collapse

The state public works department has ordered a structural audit of 1,214 bridges older than 25 years, a week after the Kesarganj bridge collapse killed nine people. Teams from two engineering colleges will inspect each bridge and submit reports by 31 January. Bridges rated unsafe will be closed to heavy vehicles. The audit is estimated to cost ₹38 crore.
